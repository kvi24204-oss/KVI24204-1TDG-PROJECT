About the Fashion Club

Leaders host practical workshops like sewing, upcycling old clothes, and making mood boards, organize school-wide clothing swaps to promote sustainability, and often put on a live catwalk fashion show to showcase their original designs.

The Fashion club provides sustainability by promoting circular clothing loops, hosting community-led garment swaps, and educating members on low-impact styling.
