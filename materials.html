Materials & Info

Satin is a luxurious fabric weave characterized by a glossy, smooth surface on one side and a dull back.

Cotton is a natural, plant-based fabric prized for its breathability, softness, and durability.

Nylon is a versatile family of synthetic polymers known as polyamides, primarily derived from crude oil.

Spandex, also known as elastane or Lycra, is a highly elastic synthetic fiber made of a polyurethane-polyurea copolymer.

Linen is a highly durable, breathable textile made from the fibers of the flax plant.

Merino material is a highly prized, luxuriously soft natural wool sourced from Merino sheep.
